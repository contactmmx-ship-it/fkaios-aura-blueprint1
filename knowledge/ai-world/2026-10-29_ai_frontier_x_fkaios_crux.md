## 4. Memory vs learning vs improvement (rule FKAIOS must follow)
- Information: obtained from a source.
- Memory: stored for later.
- Learning: a past outcome changes a decision, strategy or procedure.
- Measured improvement: a comparable next task is demonstrably better.
- Broader intelligence: skills transfer across tasks (open research problem).
Saving a chat is memory, not learning. Claim learning only when the next comparable job measurably improves.
